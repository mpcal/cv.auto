Machine Learning Engineer

Fjordlight Labs · Oslo or remote (Europe) · Full-time

Fjordlight Labs builds computer-vision software that inspects wind turbine blades from drone footage. We are hiring a Machine Learning Engineer to take our models from research to production.

What you will do

  • Train, evaluate and deploy deep-learning models for defect detection.
  • Own our MLOps stack and model monitoring.

What we are looking for

  • 3+ years of experience building machine learning systems in production.
  • Strong Python skills and experience with PyTorch.
  • Hands-on experience with Docker and Kubernetes.
  • Experience with MLflow or a similar experiment-tracking tool.
  • Good communication skills in English.
  • Experience with computer vision is a plus.
