Grandma's apple cake

Ingredients: 4 apples, 200 g flour, 150 g sugar, 3 eggs, 125 g butter, 1 packet of baking powder, cinnamon.

Peel and slice the apples. Cream the butter with the sugar, add the eggs one by one, then fold in the flour and
the baking powder. Pour the batter into a greased tin, cover it with the apple slices and sprinkle with cinnamon.
Bake at 180 °C for about 45 minutes, until golden brown. Let it cool before serving with whipped cream.
